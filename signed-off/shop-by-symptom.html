Symptoms A to Z

Know what you are seeing? Go straight to it.

Can't see it? Email us ›

Back & spine

  • Hunched or arched posture
  • Reluctant to jump or climb
  • Sudden rear weakness
  • Wobbly back legs
  • Yelping when touched

Behaviour & mood

  • Destructive behaviour
  • Hiding or unusually clingy
  • Pacing or restlessness
  • Trembling at noises
  • Whining or barking when alone

Eyes & ears

  • Head shaking
  • Odour from the ears
  • Red, inflamed ear flaps
  • Scratching at ears
  • Weepy or red eyes

Legs & paws

  • Holding a paw up
  • Licking or chewing paws
  • Limping or favouring a leg
  • Scuffed nails or dragging paws
  • Slow to get up
  • Stiffness after rest

Mouth & teeth

  • Bad breath
  • Bleeding or red gums
  • Dropping food or slow chewing
  • Pawing at the mouth
  • Yellow or brown tartar

Skin & coat

  • Constant licking of one spot
  • Excessive scratching
  • Flaky skin or dandruff
  • Hair loss or bald patches
  • Raw, weepy hot spots
  • Red or irritated skin

Tummy & gut

  • Appetite changes
  • Excessive wind
  • Gurgling, noisy stomach
  • Loose stools or diarrhoea
  • Vomiting or regurgitation
  • Weight gain

Whole body

  • Drinking more than usual
  • General stiffness with age
  • Low energy or lethargy
  • Recovering from surgery or injury
  • Trouble managing weight
