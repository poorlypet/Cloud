# Arthritis and stiff joints: 60 s conversion ad (kraft scrapbook)

`out/poorly-pet-joints-60.mp4` is the finished film. It is 1080x1920 (9:16), 30 fps and 60.0 s long, encoded as H.264 with AAC audio at -14.1 LUFS integrated and a -2.7 dBTP true peak.

Prices correct at time of making (1 Oct 2026); check before running the ad.

## Look

The film looks like a kraft scrapbook journal. The base is warm kraft-brown paper made from layered SVG `feTurbulence` (mottling, light patches, fibre streaks and grain) plus about 500 short fibre strokes from a fixed hash. It is rasterised once at load, so every render is identical. On top of it sit:

- cream card stock and torn-edge strips, held down by semi-transparent washi tape in mint, rust, cream and dots;
- headings in Caveat Brush, body text in Patrick Hand and prices in Kalam 700;
- rust rubber-stamp impressions with an ink-speckle filter, which thunk down and bump the page;
- polaroid product cards with hand-drawn object illustrations and manila price tags swinging on twine from a brass brad.

The coupon code POORLY10 is stamped in Inter 800, a print font, so the "10" cannot be misread as "IO". The Poorly Pet mark and wordmark close the film.

Each new page slides in from the right like a turned scrapbook spread: it has a shadow on its leading edge, and the old page drifts left and darkens. Before some turns, the cards are un-taped (the tape peels back) and slide off. No animals or people are drawn anywhere. The illustrations are objects only: a joint icon, tub, pad, bottle, pouch, jar, coat, ramp, capsule, house, van and shield.

## Files

- `index.html` is the film. `renderFrame(t)` is a pure function of time (springs from `lib/motion.js` only; no CSS transitions, timers or `Math.random`). Opening the file in a browser plays a looping preview.
- `cues.js` is the single timeline. It holds the page starts and every event (write, draw, card, stamp, tag, peel, press) with its sound. The film and the score both read it.
- `audio/score.py` builds `audio/score.wav`, an 84 BPM score with felt piano, Rhodes comping, a walking upright bass and brushes. It lifts into the offer (brighter voicings, the melody up an octave, a soft kick) and resolves to Fmaj9 on the button press. Every sound is cue-driven: pen scratch, doodle scratch, tick, card press, tape tear, tape peel, stamp thunk, price ding, page slide, marker, stars, logo pop and button click. The script prints the loudness and each cue type's worst "sfx peak over music RMS"; every type is at +7.6 dB or more.
- `render.mjs` renders stills (`--stills 1,4.5`), one frame per beat (`--beats`), or the full film muxed with the score.
- `fonts/` holds Caveat Brush, Patrick Hand, Kalam 700 (Google Fonts, latin subset) and Inter, all bundled locally.

## Rebuild

```
ln -sfn <node_modules with playwright-core + ffmpeg-static> node_modules
python3 audio/score.py
CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium node render.mjs
```
