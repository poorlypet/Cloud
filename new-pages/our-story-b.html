Built for Marble. Here for every dog.

Our Dachshund was diagnosed with IVDD at three. Helping her is how Poorly Pet began, from home in Sandbach, Cheshire.

Shop our IVDD rangeRead the story
2024

It started with Marble

In 2024, at just three years old, our much-loved Dachshund Marble was diagnosed with IVDD. We had first noticed her dragging her back legs and losing her balance. Almost overnight we were learning words like “disc extrusion” and “crate rest”.

2024

The search for better support

We couldn’t find the right help, so we kept looking. Late nights reading studies, forums and vet guidance taught us one thing: good products existed, but finding the right ones was far too hard.

2025

Learning, testing and helping

We tested products, read everything we could and kept what genuinely helped. With patience and careful monitoring, Marble’s better days started to outnumber her bad ones, and we began sharing what we had learned with other dog owners.

End of 2025

Poorly Pet was born

At the end of 2025, Joe and Lily founded Poorly Pet from home in Sandbach, Cheshire: one place for guidance and carefully chosen products for recovery, comfort and everyday wellbeing.

Today

Here for every dog

Products from 73 brands, sorted by condition and by symptom. Every product we stock exists because it would have helped Marble.
