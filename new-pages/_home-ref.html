Symptom focus: itchy skin

What it is, what is behind it and what actually helps.

Shop itchy skin ›

Ask us about itchy skin.

An over-reaction to something ordinary. Pollen, grass, dust mites or a food. The skin gets inflamed, the dog scratches, the scratching makes it worse.
Scratching that wakes them at night. One paw licked rusty brown. Pink skin on the belly and armpits. Flakes on the bed.
Omega 3 every day to calm the skin from inside. A gentle wash and a balm on the outside. A flea routine underneath it all. Most dogs settle in two to three weeks.
Sudden swelling or hives, a face that puffs up, round bald patches, or a hot weepy patch that spreads. Today, not tomorrow.

Condition focus: IVDD

What it is, what to watch for and what actually helps.

Shop IVDD support ›

Ask us about IVDD.

A disc in the spine bulging or bursting and pressing on the cord. Dachshunds, Bassets, Corgis and Beagles are most at risk, but any dog can get it.
A hunched back. A yelp when picked up. Reluctant to jump or do the stairs. Back legs that wobble, cross, or suddenly stop working.
Strict rest first, weeks of it. A back brace to stop twisting, ramps so there is no jumping, grip on hard floors, and a harness with a handle for the toilet trips. Keep them lean.
Sudden weakness or paralysis is a vet today, not tomorrow. So is a dog that cannot wee, or pain that is getting worse by the hour.
