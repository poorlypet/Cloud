Questions

What is the Poorly Pet Club?

Our free rewards scheme. You earn points on every order and spend them as money off the next one.

How do I join?

Create a Poorly Pet account with your email address. Your account is your membership, there is nothing else to fill in.

When are points added?

The day your order ships. You can see points on their way in your account before then.

How do I spend my points?

500 points is £5 off and 1,000 points is £10 off. You can use them on any order and there is no minimum spend.

What are the Club tiers?

Member from your first order, 5 points per £1. Regular after £150 spent, 6 points per £1 plus free delivery on every order. Committed after £400 spent, 8 points per £1, free delivery and first look at new brands.

Do members get early access to sales?

Yes. Club members see sale prices a day before everyone else.

How do birthday points work?

Add your dog’s birthday in your account and we add 250 points on the day.

Where can I see my points balance?

Sign in to your account to see your points, tier and vouchers. You can add your dog’s birthday there too.
