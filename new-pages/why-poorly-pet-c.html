Why Poorly Pet

From Marble to Poorly Pet.

How one dog’s diagnosis became one place for the right support. Here’s the journey, and what we do today.

Our journey

  1. 2024

    A diagnosis at three

    At just three years old, our Dachshund Marble was diagnosed with IVDD. We’d noticed her dragging her back legs and losing her balance.

    Almost overnight we were learning words like “disc extrusion” and “crate rest”.

    Photo: Marble
  2. 2024

    The search for better support

    We couldn’t find the right help, so we kept looking. Late nights reading studies, forums and vet guidance.

    Good products existed. Finding the right ones was far too hard.

    Photo: Marble resting
  3. 2025

    Learning, testing and helping

    We tested products, read everything we could and found what genuinely helped.

    With patience and careful monitoring, Marble’s better days started to outnumber her bad ones. We began sharing what we’d learned with other dog owners.

    Photo: Marble on a good day
  4. End of 2025

    Poorly Pet is born

    Joe and Lily founded Poorly Pet from home in Sandbach, Cheshire.

    One trusted place for vet-informed guidance, an AI Symptom Scanner and carefully chosen products for recovery, comfort and everyday wellbeing.

    Photo: Joe and Lily with Marble
  5. Today

    Built for Marble. Here for every dog.

    Products from 73 brands, sorted by condition and by symptom. Guides, a dog health quiz and the AI Symptom Scanner to help you find a clearer next step.

    If we would not give it to Marble, we will not sell it.

    Photo: packing orders
