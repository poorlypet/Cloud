Order or delivery

  • Orders are usually dispatched within 1 to 2 working days.
  • Standard delivery usually arrives 2 to 4 working days after dispatch.
  • Where tracking is available, we email you a tracking link when your order is dispatched.
  • Free standard UK delivery on orders over £39. We deliver within the UK.
  • Want to change an order? Email before 1pm and we'll aim to update it the same working day, as long as it hasn't been dispatched.

Returns and refunds

  • You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order, then have a further 14 days to send it back.
  • Items should be unused and in their original packaging where possible.
  • Opened supplements, consumable health products and perishables can't be returned unless faulty.
  • We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the return.
  • Faulty, damaged or wrong item? We'll replace it or refund you in full, including return postage.
  • Eligible products also have our 30-day money-back guarantee.

Product advice

  • Every product page lists the conditions and symptoms it is designed to support.
  • The dog health quiz asks seven quick questions and matches your answers to products.
  • Still not sure? Tell us your dog's age, breed and what you're seeing and we'll suggest what might help.
  • If your dog is on medication or has a diagnosed condition, check with your vet before starting a new supplement.

Wheelchair sizing

  • Wheelchairs and other sized products have a size chart on the product page.
  • Measure your dog carefully before you order, using the measurements the chart asks for.
  • Between sizes? Send us your dog's weight, breed and measurements and we'll tell you which to choose.
  • A side-on photo of your dog standing helps us check the fit.
