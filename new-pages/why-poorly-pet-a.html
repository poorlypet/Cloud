Marble's story

Chapter 12024

The diagnosis

In 2024, at just three years old, Marble was diagnosed with IVDD.

We first noticed her dragging her back legs and losing her balance. Almost overnight we were learning words like “disc extrusion” and “crate rest”.

What is IVDD?

A disc in the spine bulging or bursting and pressing on the cord. Dachshunds are among the most at risk, but any dog can get it. Sudden weakness or paralysis is a vet today, not tomorrow.

Photo: Marble

Chapter 22024

The search

We couldn’t find the right help, so we kept looking.

Late nights reading studies, owner forums and vet guidance taught us one thing. Good products existed, but finding the right ones was far too hard.

Photo: Marble resting

Chapter 32025

Better days

We tested products, read everything we could and found what genuinely helped.

With patience and a lot of careful monitoring, Marble’s better days started to outnumber her bad ones. We began sharing what we’d learned with other dog owners.

Photo: Marble on a good day

Chapter 4End of 2025

Poorly Pet is born

At the end of 2025, Joe and Lily founded Poorly Pet from home in Sandbach, Cheshire.

One trusted place for vet-informed guidance, an AI Symptom Scanner and carefully chosen products for recovery, comfort and everyday wellbeing.

Built for Marble. Here for every dog.
