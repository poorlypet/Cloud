The more often, the more you save

Your discount applies to every delivery, for as long as you subscribe.

How it works

Set it up once on the product page, then leave it to us.

  1. Choose SubscribeOn any eligible product, choose Subscribe instead of a one-off purchase.
  2. Pick how oftenEvery 2, 4 or 8 weeks. The more often, the bigger the discount.
  3. We remind you firstWe email a few days before each order, so you have time to skip or change it.
  4. It arrives on scheduleEach order is charged and sent automatically, with the same delivery and tracking as any order.

Skip, pause or cancel

You stay in control. There is no minimum term and no fee to change or cancel.

Skip an order
Plenty left? Skip the next delivery and the one after carries on as normal.
Pause
Going away? Pause your subscription and pick it back up when you are ready.
Change how often
Switch between every 2, 4 or 8 weeks. Your discount changes to match.
Swap or add products
Swap an item for another eligible product, or add extras to your next order.
Cancel
Any time, with no fees and no notice period. If the next order has already been charged, it is still sent, then no more are created.
Where to do it
Sign in and go to Subscriptions in your account, use the link in your order confirmation email, or email us and we will do it for you. Changes need to be made before your next billing date.
