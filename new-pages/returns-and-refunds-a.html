Returns and refunds

14 days to change your mind, and faulty items always put right.

Start a return

Returns at a glance

The time limits, and how to send something back.

14 daysTo tell us you want to return, from the day it arrives
14 daysTo send it back, from the day you tell us
14 daysTo get your refund, to your original payment method

Return postage is paid by you, unless the item is faulty, damaged or wrong.

How to return

  1. Email us within 14 daysSend your order number and the items to hello@poorly-pet.com.
  2. We reply with next stepsWithin one working day, including where to send it.
  3. Post it back within 14 daysUnused, in its original packaging where possible. Keep your proof of postage.
  4. Get your refundWithin 14 days of us receiving it, or your proof of postage.
Start a return

What you can return

Most things, as long as they’re unused.

You can return

  • Unused items in their original condition, within 14 days
  • Anything faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, even if opened

We can’t take back

  • Supplements, once opened or the seal is broken
  • Other consumable health products, once opened
  • Perishable items, once opened or the seal is broken

Unless they are faulty. This is for hygiene and safety, and doesn’t affect your statutory rights.

Faulty, damaged or wrong?

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, we’ll send a replacement or give you a full refund, including return postage. This covers opened supplements too. Email your order number and a photo if you can.
