Delivery costs and times

We send UK orders by standard tracked delivery with Evri. Delivery is free on orders over £39; below that it costs £3.99. The same prices apply to every UK address.

Dispatch and working days

Orders are typically dispatched within 1 to 2 working days. Standard delivery then usually arrives 2 to 4 working days after dispatch, so allow 3 to 6 working days in total.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. Please allow extra time during busy periods and around bank holidays.

Where we deliver

  • England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

    We deliver to addresses across the United Kingdom, at the same price wherever you are, including the Highlands and islands and Northern Ireland.

  • Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man

    These aren’t part of our UK delivery area at checkout. Please email hello@poorly-pet.com before you order.

  • Outside the UK

    We currently deliver within the UK only. If you would like to order from outside the UK, please email hello@poorly-pet.com first.

Wheelchairs and larger items

Wheelchairs and other larger items are delivered at the same prices as everything else, so they’re free on orders over £39.

Some mobility products, such as made-to-order braces and supports and items from our specialist EU partners, are made or sent by the supplier to their own timescales. Where this applies, the expected timeframe is shown on the product page, and the item may arrive in a separate parcel.

Our FreedomRoll wheelchair arrives needing assembly. Wheelchairs are sized to your dog, so please measure before you order: each product page explains how.

Ordering from outside the UK?

We deliver to UK addresses only. Email hello@poorly-pet.com before you order and we’ll let you know.

Missed deliveries and address changes

  • If you miss your delivery

    If a parcel comes back to us because of a missed delivery or an incorrect address, we’ll contact you to arrange redelivery. Redelivery may incur an additional charge.

  • If you need to change the address

    If your order hasn’t been dispatched yet, email hello@poorly-pet.com straight away with your order number and we’ll do our best to update it. Once it has been dispatched, we can’t change it.

  • Before you pay

    Please make sure your delivery address is correct at checkout.

Damaged, late or lost parcels

  • It hasn’t arrived in time

    Check the Evri tracking link in your dispatch email first. If your order still hasn’t arrived within the expected timeframe, email hello@poorly-pet.com with your order number and we’ll put it right.

  • Tracking says delivered, but you can’t find it

    Check any safe place around your home and ask your neighbours. If it still hasn’t turned up, email us with your order number.

  • It arrived damaged or faulty, or it’s the wrong item

    Email us with your order number; a photo of the item and packaging helps. We’ll arrange a replacement or a full refund, including any return postage. See returns and refunds.

Common questions

Do you offer next-day or express delivery?

No. We offer standard tracked UK delivery with Evri: £3.99, or free on orders over £39. It is usually dispatched within 1 to 2 working days and arrives 2 to 4 working days after that.

Is my parcel tracked?

Where tracking is available, we email you the Evri tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also use Track my order.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, if the order hasn’t been dispatched. Email hello@poorly-pet.com as soon as possible with your order number. Once it has been dispatched we can’t change it.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

We currently deliver to UK addresses only. If you would like to order from outside the UK, please email us first.

Can I collect my order?

No. We are an online-only store, so every order is delivered.

Do subscription orders ship the same way?

Yes. Subscription orders are dispatched on the same schedule as one-off orders, with the same delivery prices and timescales.

Why has only part of my order arrived?

Some items, such as made-to-order braces and supports and items from our specialist EU partners, are sent by the supplier and may arrive in a separate parcel. The product page shows their timeframe.

Is delivery more expensive for wheelchairs?

No. Wheelchairs and other larger items use the same delivery prices as everything else.
