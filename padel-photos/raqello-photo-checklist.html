PhotosJPG, sRGB, quality 80. Keep each under 500 KB where you can. Shopify makes the smaller sizes itself.
VideosMP4 (H.264), no sound, 10 to 20 seconds, under 20 MB. Upload in Content › Files or straight into the section.
NamesLower case, hyphens, no spaces. Shopify keeps the file name, so it also helps Google image search.
LogosSVG or transparent PNG. Brand press kits have them, ask the brand rep if not.
