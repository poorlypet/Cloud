    Size guide

    How to measure your dog, and the sizes for coats, collars, harnesses and beds.

    01

    How to measure

    • Back length: from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, with your dog standing.
    • Chest: around the widest part, just behind the front legs.
    • Neck: where a collar sits, leaving room for two fingers.
    • Length for beds: from nose to the base of the tail while lying down, then add 15 to 20cm.

    Use a soft tape. If your dog is between sizes, choose the larger.
