    Accessibility

    We want everyone to be able to shop with us comfortably, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

    Last updated 12 November 2026

    On this pageOur aimWhat we’ve doneKnown issuesGetting help

    Our aim

    We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That means clear text, good contrast, everything usable with a keyboard, and descriptions for images.

    What we’ve done

    • Text and backgrounds have strong contrast, and you can zoom the page to 200% without losing anything.
    • Every page works with a keyboard and with screen readers.
    • Images have text descriptions, and video plays without sound.
    • If you’ve asked your device to reduce motion, our animations switch off.

    Known issues

    Some content from other companies, such as payment options and review widgets, may not fully meet the standard. We’re working with them to improve it.

    Getting help

    If anything on the site is difficult to use, or you need information in a different format, email [hello@axelandhound.co.uk] and we’ll help you place your order another way.

    Still need help?

    Our team is here Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and replies to every message within one working day.
