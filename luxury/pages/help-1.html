    Popular questions

    When will my order arrive?

    Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and is free on orders over £100. Next-day delivery is £12 when you order by 2pm on a working day.

    How do I return something?

    You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items with their tags. Start a return from your account or the link in your dispatch email. Returns are free.

    How do I measure my dog?

    Measure the back from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, the chest around the widest part behind the front legs, and the neck where a collar sits.

    Can I swap for a different size?

    Yes. Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    How do I wash a coat?

    Most coats can be machine washed at 30°C. Close all fastenings first and leave to dry flat. Do not tumble dry.
