    A letter from Sam & Alex

    Beautiful things for dogs, and the homes they share.

    Dear reader,

    It started with two dogs and a long search.

    Biscuit, a miniature dachshund with a very long back. Juno, a whippet who shivers at the first sign of autumn.

    Everything we found for them was either beautifully made or practical. Almost never both.

    So we went looking for the people who could do both.

    Photo, 3:2, Biscuit and Juno

    What we collect is simple to describe and hard to find.

    Beds: deep, soft and made to be washed, because they will be.

    Coats: cut for real shapes: long backs, deep chests, narrow waists.

    Walking: leather, rope and waxed cotton that soften with every walk.

    Dining: stoneware and oak that belong on a kitchen floor.

    Play: wool felt and natural rubber, sturdy enough to last.

    Travel: carriers and beds for weekends away and long drives home.

    Photo, 3:2, around the house

    We look for things that are made to last, made well and made beautifully, and then we live with them first.

    The makers we work with are some of the best in Europe.

    In Germany, Cloud7. Coats and beds from a Berlin studio that has dressed city dogs for twenty years.

    In Denmark, Hollow & Hearth. Wool blankets and bedding woven on the coast north of Copenhagen.

    In Italy, Clay & Kiln. Stoneware bowls thrown and glazed by hand outside Milan.

    In France, Maison Lune. Bouclé and linen beds stitched in a small atelier in Lyon.

    In Netherlands, Fold & Field. Waxed cotton coats and leads from a workshop in Utrecht.

    In Scotland, Bridle & Co. Bridle leather collars cut and stitched in the Scottish borders.

    Film, 3:2, a workshop visit

    We visit every workshop before we stock a single piece.

    We buy in small runs, so nothing sits in a warehouse for years.

    We work on colours and fits together, some only made for us.

    And we stay. Most of our makers have been with us since the start.

    We pack every order by hand, wrap it in tissue and answer every email ourselves. Thank you for being part of it.

    With love, Sam & Alex
