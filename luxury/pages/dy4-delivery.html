    Delivery FAQs

    Can I change my delivery address?

    Yes, as long as your order hasn’t been dispatched yet. Reply to your order confirmation email, or contact us, and we’ll update it for you.

    What if my parcel hasn’t arrived?

    Check the tracking link in your dispatch email first. If it’s late or missing, contact us and we’ll send a replacement or refund you in full.
