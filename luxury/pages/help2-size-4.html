    Questions

    Good to know

    How do I measure my dog?

    Measure the back from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, the chest around the widest part behind the front legs, and the neck where a collar sits.

    My dog is between sizes

    Choose the larger size, especially if your dog will wear a jumper underneath.

    Do sizes vary between brands?

    Yes, a little. Every product page has its own chart, so always check the measurements there.

    Can I exchange if the size is wrong?

    Yes, exchanges are free within 14 days.
