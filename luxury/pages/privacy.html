    Privacy policy

    How we collect, use and look after your personal information, and the rights you have over it.

    Last updated 12 November 2026

    Who we are

    This website is run by Axel & Hound Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number [00000000]), with its registered office at [address]. We are the controller of your personal data, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for keeping it safe.

    If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at [hello@axelandhound.co.uk].

    What we collect

    We only collect what we need to run the shop and look after you:

    • Your name, email address, phone number and delivery and billing addresses, when you place an order or create an account.
    • Your order history, returns and any messages you send us.
    • Payment details, which are handled securely by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card number.
    • Information about how you use our website, such as pages viewed and the device you use, collected through cookies if you agree to them.
    • Your dog’s name and size, if you choose to tell us, to help with sizing.

    To process, deliver and support your order, including returns and refundsPerforming our contract with you
    To send emails about your order, such as confirmations and trackingPerforming our contract with you
    To send our newsletter and offers, if you sign upYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time
    To prevent fraud and keep our website secureOur legitimate interests

    Who we share it with

    We never sell your data. We share it only with companies that help us run the shop, and only as much as they need:

    • Shopify, which hosts our website and stores order information.
    • Payment providers, including Shopify Payments, PayPal, Klarna and Apple Pay.
    • Delivery couriers, so they can deliver your order.
    • Our email provider, to send order updates and, if you’ve agreed, our newsletter.
    • Trustpilot, if you leave a review.

    Some of these companies are based outside the UK. Where your data is transferred abroad, we make sure it is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

    How long we keep it

    We keep order records for six years, as required for tax purposes. Account details are kept until you close your account. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. After that, your data is deleted or made anonymous.

    Your rights

    Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

    • See a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete.
    • Ask us to delete your data, where we no longer need to keep it.
    • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data.
    • Ask for your data in a format you can take elsewhere.
    • Withdraw your consent to marketing at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email.

    To use any of these rights, email [hello@axelandhound.co.uk]. We will reply within one month.

    Making a complaint

    If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

    Still need help?

    Our team is here Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and replies to every message within one working day.
