    Delivery and returns

    Everything you need to know about getting your order, and sending it back if it isn’t right.

    Delivery options

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day.

    OptionDelivery timeCost
    Standard2 to 4 working days£4.95, free over £100
    Next dayOrder by 2pm, Monday to Friday£12
    Nominated dayChoose a date at checkout£9.95

    Tracking your order

    As soon as your parcel leaves us we’ll email you a tracking link. You can also see every order in your account.

    Returns

    You have 14 days from delivery to send items back, unworn and with their tags. Returns are free.

    1. Start your returnFrom your account, or the link in your dispatch email.
    2. Pack it upUnworn, with tags, in the original packaging if you can.
    3. Drop it offAt any post office or parcel shop. We’ll email you when it arrives.

    Exchanges

    Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    Refunds

    We refund to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return reaching us.

    Common questions

    When will my order arrive?

    Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and is free on orders over £100. Next-day delivery is £12 when you order by 2pm on a working day.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    We can change or cancel an order within an hour of it being placed. Contact us as soon as you can with your order number.

    Do you deliver outside the UK?

    At the moment we deliver to the UK only.

    How do I return something?

    You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items with their tags. Start a return from your account or the link in your dispatch email. Returns are free.

    When will I get my refund?

    Refunds are made to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return reaching us.

    Can I swap for a different size?

    Yes. Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.
