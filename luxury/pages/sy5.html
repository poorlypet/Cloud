    Axel & Hound

    Beautiful things for dogs, and the homes they share.

    Beautifully made.

    Or practical.

    We wanted both, so we went looking.

    It started with two dogs and a long search.

    Biscuit, a miniature dachshund with a very long back. Juno, a whippet who shivers at the first sign of autumn.

    Everything we found for them was either beautifully made or practical. Almost never both.

    So we went looking for the people who could do both.
