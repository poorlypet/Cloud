    UK delivery

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day leave us the same day. Working days are Monday to Friday, not including bank holidays.

    Beds & larger pieces

    Beds and other large pieces travel by our specialist courier and may need a signature. You’ll get a one-hour delivery window by text on the morning of delivery, and you can rearrange it from the text if the time doesn’t suit. Delivery costs are the same as above.

    International delivery

    We ship to most countries worldwide, and every international order is tracked. Delivery options, costs and times for your address are shown at checkout before you pay.

    • Orders sent outside the UK may be charged import duties and taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the recipient on delivery and aren’t included in our prices.
    • Delivery times are from dispatch and can be longer during customs checks or local holidays.

    Tracking your order

    Your dispatch email has a tracking link that follows your order to your door. You can also track it with your order number and email address, or see every order in your account.

    Delivery FAQs

    When will my order leave you?

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are packed and sent the same day. Anything after 2pm, or at the weekend, goes the next working day.

    Do you ship worldwide?

    Yes, we ship to most countries. International delivery costs and times are shown at checkout once you enter your address. Orders outside the UK may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country, which are paid by the recipient.

    Can I change my delivery address?

    Yes, as long as your order hasn’t been dispatched yet. Reply to your order confirmation email, or contact us, and we’ll update it for you.

    What if my order hasn’t arrived?

    Check the tracking link in your dispatch email first. If your order still hasn’t arrived, or looks lost, contact us and we’ll investigate with the courier straight away. If it can’t be found, we’ll send a replacement or refund you in full.

    What if my order arrives damaged?

    Contact us within 14 days with a photo of the damage and the packaging. We’ll arrange a replacement or a full refund, and collect the damaged item at no cost to you.

    What if I’m out when it arrives?

    The courier will leave it in your safe place or with a neighbour. If neither is possible, they’ll leave a card and try again, or you can collect from a local drop-off point.

    Still need help?

    Our team is here Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and replies to every message within one working day.
