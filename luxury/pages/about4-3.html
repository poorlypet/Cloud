    About us

    Beautiful things for dogs, and the homes they share.

    Axel & Hound began with two dogs and a simple frustration. Biscuit, a miniature dachshund with a very long back, and Juno, a whippet who feels the cold, needed coats, beds and collars that actually fitted them.

    The problem

    Everything we found was either beautifully made or practical. Rarely both. The beautiful things fell apart, and the practical things looked like they belonged in a kennel.

    The search

    So we went looking. We found small makers in Berlin, Bristol, Copenhagen and the Scottish borders who build things to last, and who care as much about the dog as the design.

    The test

    Every piece we stock is lived with at home first. Biscuit tests the beds, Juno tests the coats, and if it doesn’t survive a muddy winter, it doesn’t make the cut.

    The shop

    We’re still small, on purpose. We pack every order ourselves, wrap it in tissue, and answer every email. When you buy from us, you’re buying from people who have read the label, washed the coat and watched their own dog fall asleep on the bed.

    Sam & Alex
