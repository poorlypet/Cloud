    Questions

    Good to know

    When will my order arrive?

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Standard delivery then takes 2 to 4 working days, next day arrives the following working day.

    Do you deliver everywhere in the UK?

    Yes. The Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands can take 1 to 2 extra working days, and next-day delivery isn’t available there.

    What if my order is late?

    We will always deliver within 30 days of your order unless we agree another date with you, as the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. If we don’t, you can cancel and we’ll refund you in full.

    What if it arrives damaged or goes missing?

    Your order is our responsibility until it reaches you. Contact us and we’ll send a replacement or refund you straight away.

    Do you deliver outside the UK?

    Not at the moment. We hope to soon.

    Nothing here affects your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
