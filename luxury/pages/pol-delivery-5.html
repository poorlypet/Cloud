    Prices are for UK mainland addresses. Working days are Monday to Friday, not including bank holidays.

    When your order will arrive

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2pm, at the weekend or on a bank holiday are dispatched the next working day. We’ll email you as soon as your parcel leaves us.

    Where we deliver

    We deliver to every UK address. Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands can take 1 to 2 extra working days, and next-day delivery isn’t available there. We don’t deliver outside the UK at the moment.

    Tracking your parcel

    Every order is tracked. Your dispatch email has a link to follow it, or you can track your order here with your order number and email address.

    Late, missing or damaged parcels

    We aim to deliver within the times above. Unless we agree a different date with you, we will always deliver within 30 days of your order, as the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. If we don’t, you can cancel and we’ll refund you in full.

    Your order is our responsibility until it reaches you, or someone you’ve asked to accept it. If it arrives damaged, or hasn’t arrived after the expected date, contact us and we’ll replace it or refund you.

    Large items

    Beds and larger pieces travel by courier and may need a signature. The courier will text you a one-hour delivery window on the day.

    Still have a question?

    A real person will reply within one working day, Monday to Friday.
