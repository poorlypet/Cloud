    Your right to cancel

    Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day after you receive it. You then have a further 14 days to return the goods. We’ll refund you within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of you showing us proof you’ve sent them, including the standard delivery cost of your original order.

After that, we’ll repair or replace them, and if that doesn’t work, you’re entitled to a refund. Nothing in these terms affects these rights.
