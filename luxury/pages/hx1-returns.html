    Exchanges

    Need a different size or colour? Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new one as soon as yours is on its way back, at no extra cost.

    Your refund

    We refund you within 14 days of receiving your return, or of you showing us proof you’ve sent it, whichever is sooner. Your refund includes the standard delivery cost of the original order. If you paid for a faster option, we refund the standard cost only.

    The condition of returns

    You can handle and try things on as you would in a shop. If an item has been worn on a walk, washed, or had its tags removed, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in its value.

    What we can’t take back

    • Personalised or engraved items, unless they’re faulty
    • Grooming products such as shampoos and balms, once the seal is broken
    • Treats and food, once opened

    Faulty or not as described

    Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 come first. You can reject a faulty item for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After that, we’ll repair or replace it, and if that isn’t possible or the fault comes back, you can have a refund. For six months after delivery we’ll assume the fault was there when it arrived.

    Cancelling by email

    If you prefer, email hello@axelandhound.co.uk with: “I cancel my contract for the sale of the following goods: [items]. Ordered on [date] / received on [date]. Name, address and date.”

    Good to know

    How long do I have to return something?

    14 days from the day after you receive your order to tell us, then a further 14 days to send it back. This is your right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

    Are returns free?

    Yes, on every UK order. We send you a prepaid label.

    When will I get my refund?

    Within 14 days of us receiving the items, or of you showing proof you’ve sent them, whichever is sooner. We refund the standard delivery cost of the original order too.

    What can’t be returned?

    Personalised items, sealed grooming products once opened, and treats once opened, unless they’re faulty.

    What if something is faulty?

    Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you can reject it for a full refund within 30 days. After that we’ll repair or replace it, and if that doesn’t work you can have a refund.

    Can I swap for a different size?

    Yes. Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    Customer care

    Still have a question?

    A real person will reply within one working day, Monday to Friday.
