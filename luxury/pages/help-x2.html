    Orders and delivery

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. You’ll get an email with tracking as soon as your parcel leaves us.

    OptionDelivery timeCost

    When will my order arrive?

    Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and is free on orders over £100. Next-day delivery is £12 when you order by 2pm on a working day.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    We can change or cancel an order within an hour of it being placed. Contact us as soon as you can with your order number.

    Do you deliver outside the UK?

    At the moment we deliver to the UK only.

    Returns and exchanges

    You have 14 days from delivery to send something back. Returns are free, and we refund to your original payment method within 5 working days of receiving it.

    1. Start your returnFrom your account, or the link in your dispatch email.
    2. Pack it upUnworn, with tags, in the original packaging if you can.
    3. Drop it offAt any post office or parcel shop. We’ll email you when it arrives.
    How do I return something?

    You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items with their tags. Start a return from your account or the link in your dispatch email. Returns are free.

    When will I get my refund?

    Refunds are made to your original payment method within 5 working days of your return reaching us.

    Can I swap for a different size?

    Yes. Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    Sizing and fit

    Every product page has its own size guide and a Find my fit tool. Measure your dog standing, with a soft tape:

    • Back length: base of the neck to the base of the tail.
    • Chest: around the widest part, just behind the front legs.
    • Neck: where a collar sits, with room for two fingers.

    Between sizes? Choose the larger.

    How do I measure my dog?

    Measure the back from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, the chest around the widest part behind the front legs, and the neck where a collar sits.

    My dog is between sizes

    Choose the larger size, especially if your dog will wear a jumper underneath.

    Product care

    Look after your pieces and they’ll last for years. Always check the care label first.

    • Coats: machine wash at 30°C with fastenings closed. Dry flat, never tumble dry.
    • Beds: unzip and wash the cover at 30°C. Air the cushion regularly.
    • Leather: wipe clean and condition now and then.
    How do I wash a coat?

    Most coats can be machine washed at 30°C. Close all fastenings first and leave to dry flat. Do not tumble dry.

    How do I clean a bed?

    Bed covers unzip and can be washed at 30°C. Air the inner cushion regularly.
