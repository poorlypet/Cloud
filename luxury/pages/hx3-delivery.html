    How your order is packed

    Every order is checked and wrapped by our own team. We use recyclable boxes and paper, never plastic fill, and gift orders get a handwritten note if you ask for one at checkout.

    Where we deliver

    We deliver to every address in the UK. Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands can take 1 to 2 extra working days, and next-day delivery isn’t available there.

    We don’t deliver outside the UK at the moment.

    Beds and larger pieces

    Beds and other large pieces travel by courier and may need a signature. The courier will text you a one-hour delivery window on the morning of delivery, and you can rearrange it from the text if the time doesn’t suit you.

    If you’re out

    The courier will try your safe place, then a neighbour. If neither is possible, they’ll leave a card and try again the next working day, or you can collect from a local drop-off point.

    If something goes wrong

    If your parcel is late, arrives damaged or goes missing, contact us and we’ll put it right straight away with a replacement or a refund.

    Your order is our responsibility until it reaches you. We will always deliver within 30 days of your order unless we agree another date with you, as the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. If we don’t, you can cancel and we’ll refund you in full.

    Good to know

    When will my order arrive?

    Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Standard delivery then takes 2 to 4 working days, next day arrives the following working day.

    Do you deliver everywhere in the UK?

    Yes. The Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, the Isles of Scilly and the Channel Islands can take 1 to 2 extra working days, and next-day delivery isn’t available there.

    What if my order is late?

    We will always deliver within 30 days of your order unless we agree another date with you, as the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. If we don’t, you can cancel and we’ll refund you in full.

    What if it arrives damaged or goes missing?

    Your order is our responsibility until it reaches you. Contact us and we’ll send a replacement or refund you straight away.

    Do you deliver outside the UK?

    Not at the moment. We hope to soon.
