    The details

    Made for the dachshund shape

    01

    Cut for a long back

    A longer body and a shorter chest panel follow the dachshund line, so the coat covers the whole back without bunching at the front legs.

    02

    A high, soft collar

    The collar stands up to keep the neck warm and folds down on milder days. It sits clear of the ears and never rubs.

    03

    Short belly panel

    The underside is cut short and close, so it stays dry and out of the way on short legs and long walks.
