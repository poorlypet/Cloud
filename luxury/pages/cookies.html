    Cookie policy

    What cookies we use, why we use them, and how to change your choices at any time.

    Last updated 12 November 2026

    On this pageWhat cookies areThe cookies we useCookies from other companiesChanging your choicesMore information

    What cookies are

    Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit a website. Some are needed for the site to work, and others help us understand how it’s used or show you relevant offers.

    The cookies we use

    TypeWhat they doCan you turn them off?
    Strictly necessaryKeep your basket, let you sign in and check out securelyNo, the site won’t work without them
    PreferencesRemember your choices, such as your currencyYes
    AnalyticsShow us which pages are visited, so we can improve the siteYes
    MarketingShow you relevant adverts on other websites and measure themYes

    We only use preference, analytics and marketing cookies if you agree to them when you first visit.

    Cookies from other companies

    Some cookies are set by the services we use, including Shopify, Google Analytics, Klarna, Meta and Trustpilot. Each has its own privacy policy explaining how it uses information.

    Changing your choices

    You can change your cookie choices at any time from the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings, although parts of the site may not work properly if you block necessary cookies.

    More information

    For more about how we use your personal data, see our Privacy policy. Questions? Email us at [hello@axelandhound.co.uk].
