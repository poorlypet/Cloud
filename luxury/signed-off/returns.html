    Returns & refunds

    If something isn’t quite right, sending it back should be easy. UK returns are free, and you have 14 days to change your mind.

    You can return anything unworn, with its tags, for a full refund or an exchange.

    Exchanges

    Need a different size or colour? Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new one as soon as yours is on its way back, at no extra cost.

    Your refund

    We refund you within 14 days of receiving your return, or of you showing us proof you’ve sent it, whichever is sooner. Your refund includes the standard delivery cost of the original order. If you paid for a faster option, we refund the standard cost only.

    Condition of returns

    You can handle and try things on as you would in a shop. If an item has been worn on a walk, washed, or had its tags removed, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in its value.

    What we can’t take back

    Unless they’re faulty, we can’t accept returns of:

    • Personalised or engraved items
    • Grooming products such as shampoos and balms, once the seal is broken
    • Treats and food, once opened

    Faulty or not as described

    Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 come first. You can reject a faulty item for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After that, we’ll repair or replace it, and if that isn’t possible or the fault comes back, you can have a refund. For six months after delivery we’ll assume the fault was there when it arrived.

    Returns from outside the UK

    You have the same 14 days to change your mind. Please send your return with a tracked service, as we can’t be responsible for parcels lost on the way back. Return postage isn’t covered unless the item is faulty, and we can’t refund import duties or taxes.

    Returns FAQs

    How long does a refund take?

    We refund you within 14 days of your return reaching us, and usually within 5 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

    Can I return a sale item?

    Yes. Sale items can be returned in the same way as anything else, within 14 days of delivery.

    Can I return a gift?

    Yes. We’ll refund the gift to the person who bought it, or send you an exchange or a gift card instead.

    I’ve lost my returns label

    Contact us with your order number and we’ll email you a new one.

    Still need help?

    Our team is here Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and replies to every message within one working day.
