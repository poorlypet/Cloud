    Orders

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    We can change or cancel an order within an hour of it being placed. Contact us as soon as you can with your order number.

    I haven’t had a confirmation email

    Check your junk folder first. If it isn’t there, contact us and we’ll resend it and check your order went through.

    Do I need an account to order?

    No. You can check out as a guest, and create an account afterwards to track orders and start returns.

    Delivery

    When will my order arrive?

    UK standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and is free on orders over £75. Express delivery arrives the next working day when you order by 2pm.

    Do you ship worldwide?

    Yes, to most countries. Costs and times for your address are shown at checkout, and any import duties are paid by the recipient.

    What if my order hasn’t arrived?

    Contact us and we’ll investigate with the courier straight away. If it can’t be found, we’ll send a replacement or refund you in full.

    Returns & exchanges

    How do I return something?

    You have 14 days from delivery to tell us, and another 14 to send it back unworn with its tags. UK returns are free.

    When will I get my refund?

    Within 14 days of your return reaching us, and usually within 5 working days.

    Can I swap for a different size?

    Yes. Choose exchange when you start your return and we’ll send the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    Sizing & fit

    How do I measure my dog?

    Measure the back from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, the chest around the widest part behind the front legs, and the neck where a collar sits. Every product page has its own size guide.

    My dog is between sizes

    Choose the larger size, especially if your dog will wear a jumper underneath.

    Product care

    How do I wash a coat?

    Most coats can be machine washed at 30°C. Close all fastenings first and leave to dry flat. Don’t tumble dry.

    How do I clean a bed?

    Bed covers unzip and can be washed at 30°C. Air the inner cushion regularly.

    Payments

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Shop Pay and Klarna.

    Can I spread the cost?

    Yes. Shop Pay and Klarna let you pay in 3 interest-free instalments.

    Still need help?

    Our team is here Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and replies to every message within one working day.
