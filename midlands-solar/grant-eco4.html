Government funding

ECO4 and energy supplier schemes

Under the Energy Company Obligation, larger energy suppliers paid for upgrades, sometimes including solar, in low-income homes. The scheme is being wound down.

Who it was for

Households on certain benefits

Status

Closing to new applications

Look at instead

Warm Homes: Local Grant

How it works

Three steps.

01

Already applied?

If you have an application in progress, speak to the supplier or installer handling it.

02

Starting now?

Check the Warm Homes: Local Grant first. It is the main route for funded upgrades.

03

Not eligible?

You still get 0% VAT and export payments on a system you pay for.

Who it's for

Where it stands.

ECO4 was the fourth round of the scheme. The government has said it will not be replaced like for like.

  • It covered homes on means-tested benefits with a low energy rating
  • Solar was only funded alongside other upgrades
  • New applications are no longer being taken by most suppliers
  • We'll tell you if anything else is open for your address

Questions

Good to know.

Can I still get free solar through ECO4?

For most people, no. Suppliers have stopped taking new applications as the scheme ends.

What replaced it?

There is no direct replacement. The Warm Homes: Local Grant is now the main scheme for lower-income households in England.

Is anyone offering 'free solar' genuine?

Be careful. Fully funded solar only comes through official schemes with strict rules. If someone promises it to everyone, ask which scheme it is.
