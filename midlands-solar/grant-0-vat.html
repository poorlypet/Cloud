Government funding

0% VAT on solar and batteries

Solar panels and battery storage fitted to homes are zero-rated for VAT. You don't apply for it: it comes off the price automatically.

Who gets it

Every home

What you save

The full 20% VAT

Runs until

31 March 2027

How it works

Three steps.

01

We quote without VAT

Every home price we give you already has the VAT taken off.

02

We fit your system

The zero rate covers the equipment and the installation together.

03

Nothing to claim

There are no forms and nothing to send to HMRC.

Who it's for

Who it covers.

The zero rate applies to energy-saving materials installed in residential property.

  • Solar panels fitted to your home
  • Battery storage, with solar or added on its own
  • Homes in England, Scotland and Wales
  • The rate is due to go to 5% from April 2027

Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to apply?

No. We simply charge 0% VAT on the invoice.

Does it cover a battery on its own?

Yes. Since February 2024 batteries are zero-rated whether or not they are fitted with solar panels.

What happens after March 2027?

Under current rules the rate rises to 5%, so installs completed before then avoid that cost.
