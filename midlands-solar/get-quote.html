Common questions

Before you start.

How much do solar panels cost?

It depends on your roof, how many panels fit and whether you add a battery. The online quote measures your actual roof and shows a rough cost in about a minute. We then confirm a fixed price on a short call.

How long does installation take?

Most home systems are fitted in one to two days. We handle the scaffolding, the electrical work and the paperwork for your network operator.

Do I need planning permission?

Most home solar counts as permitted development, so no application is needed. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats can be different. We check this for you before any work starts.

Is a battery worth adding?

A battery stores the power you make in the day so you can use it in the evening, which usually raises your savings. The quote shows the figures with and without one so you can compare.

What if my roof isn't suitable?

We'll tell you straight. The quote looks at the direction, size and shading of your roof. If solar won't pay back on your home, we'd rather say so than sell you a system that underperforms.
