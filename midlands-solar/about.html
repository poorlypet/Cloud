About us

Renewable energy,done properly.

Midlands Solar was set up to make switching to solar simpler, clearer and more dependable for homes and businesses across the region.

Our story

A local team,
built on trust.

We started Midlands Solar because buying solar was harder than it should be: pushy sales visits, vague numbers and installers you never heard from again.

We do it differently. Straight advice about what your roof can do, a system designed around how you use electricity, and the same Midlands team for the install and everything after it.
