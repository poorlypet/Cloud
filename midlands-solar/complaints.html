About our work

Complaints about our work

If your complaint is about the installation, the products, or the service you received from us, please contact us at enquiries@midlandssolar.co.uk on 08006546343 or by post Midlands Solar Installations LTD, Unit B2, Hilton Main Industrial Estate, Wolverhampton, WV11 2BE.

We will acknowledge your complaint and tell you how we will handle it.

About your finance

Complaints relating to finance:

If your complaint relates to the finance agreement itself, including the terms of the agreement, payments, the way the finance was explained to you, or the administration of your finance agreement, please contact Hometree Finance, a trading name of HTG Finance Limited, directly at hello@hometreefinance.co.uk

If you are unhappy with the outcome of your complaint, or you have not received a final response within eight weeks, you may be able to refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS). The service is free and independent.
