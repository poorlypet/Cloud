Agricultural solar

Put your biggest roofs to work.

Barns, grain stores and livestock buildings offer large roofs with little shading. Solar cuts the cost of running refrigeration, ventilation, drying and pumping.

  • Systems sized to seasonal and daily demand
  • Suitable for steel, fibre-cement and tiled roofs
  • Three-phase supplies and larger grid connections
  • Battery storage to cover early mornings and evenings
