Questions

Paying for solar.

When do I pay?

If you pay in full, a deposit secures your install date and the balance is due once the system is installed and working. If you spread the cost, your agreement with the lender sets out the payment dates.

Will the savings cover a monthly payment?

It depends on your roof, your usage and the term you choose. We show the expected monthly saving next to the monthly payment so you can compare them directly.

Who am I borrowing from?

The lender named on this page. They carry out the credit check and you sign your agreement with them.

Does a battery change the numbers?

Usually, yes. A battery costs more up front but lets you use more of your own power, which raises the saving. The quote shows both versions.
