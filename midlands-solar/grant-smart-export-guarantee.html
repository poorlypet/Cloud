Government funding

Smart Export Guarantee

When your panels make more than you're using, the spare power goes to the grid. Under the Smart Export Guarantee an energy supplier pays you for every unit.

Who gets it

Any home with an MCS install

What you get

Paid per unit exported

Paid by

Your chosen supplier

How it works

Three steps.

01

We fit and certify

After the install you receive an MCS certificate for your system.

02

You pick a tariff

Compare export tariffs and apply to the supplier you choose. It doesn't have to be your own supplier.

03

You get paid

Your smart meter records what you export and the supplier pays you for it.

Who it's for

What you need.

Most homes we fit qualify straight away.

  • An MCS-certified installation (ours are)
  • A smart meter that records exports every half hour
  • A system of up to 5MW, which covers every home
  • Rates differ between suppliers, so it pays to compare
Check if I qualify →

Questions

Good to know.

How much will I be paid?

It depends on the tariff. Suppliers set their own rates, and some pay much more than others, especially to their own customers.

Do I have to switch supplier?

No. You can keep your current supplier for the power you buy and use a different one for export.

Is a battery still worth it?

Usually. A battery lets you use more of your own power, which is worth more per unit than exporting it. The quote shows both.
