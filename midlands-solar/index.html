The solar quote

Your roof, measured from above.

Most solar quotes start with a salesperson in your living room. Ours starts with your postcode and takes about a minute.

STEP 1

Enter your postcode

Pick your address and drop the pin on your roof. That's all we need from you to start.

STEP 2

We map your roof

Satellite data measures each side of your roof: its size, its angle, which way it faces and how much sun it gets through the year.

STEP 3

Panels go where the sun is

You see the panels on your own roof. Add or remove them, pick a side, and choose whether to add a battery.

STEP 4

See your price, then talk to us

You get a rough cost and your likely savings straight away. Book a short call and we confirm a fixed price.
