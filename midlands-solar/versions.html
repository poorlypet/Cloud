How much do solar panels cost?

It depends on your roof and whether you add a battery. The online quote measures your roof and shows a rough cost in about a minute.

How long does installation take?

Most home systems are fitted in one to two days.

Do I need planning permission?

Usually not. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats can be different, and we check before work starts.
