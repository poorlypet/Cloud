Questions

Funding, answered.

Is there a free solar panel scheme?

Not for everyone. Fully funded solar is limited to households that meet income, benefits or energy-rating rules. Most customers pay for their system and benefit from 0% VAT and export payments.

Do I have to apply for 0% VAT?

No. It applies automatically to solar panels and batteries fitted to homes, and it's already in the price we quote.

How do I get paid for exported power?

After your install you receive an MCS certificate. You choose an export tariff from a supplier and they pay you for what your smart meter records going back to the grid.

Can you check what I qualify for?

Yes. Tell us your postcode and a little about your household and we'll tell you which schemes are open to you.
