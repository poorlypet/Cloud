Government funding

Help with the cost of solar.

There's no single solar grant for everyone, but most homes get some help. Here's what's available and who it's for.

For every home

Support you get automatically.

Everyone
0% VAT

No VAT on home solar and batteries

Solar panels and battery storage fitted to homes are zero-rated for VAT until 31 March 2027. It's already built into our prices, so there's nothing to claim.

Everyone with an MCS install
SEG

Get paid for the power you export

Under the Smart Export Guarantee, energy suppliers pay you for electricity you send back to the grid. You need an MCS-certified installation and a smart meter. Rates vary by supplier, so it's worth comparing tariffs.

For some households

Grants, if you qualify.

These schemes are aimed at lower-income households and homes that are expensive to heat. Rules, budgets and closing dates change, so we check what's open for your address before you commit to anything.

Income and EPC based

Warm Homes: Local Grant

Funding for energy upgrades, which can include solar panels, for eligible households in England. It's delivered through participating local councils, and eligibility depends on household income and your home's energy rating.

Benefits and income based

Energy supplier schemes

Larger energy suppliers have funded home upgrades for eligible households under the Energy Company Obligation. What's covered and who qualifies has changed over time, so ask us what applies now.

This page is a guide, not financial advice. Government schemes change and some depend on your council. We'll confirm exactly what you can claim when we quote.

Questions

Funding, answered.

Is there a free solar panel scheme?

Not for everyone. Fully funded solar is limited to households that meet income, benefits or energy-rating rules under specific schemes. Most customers pay for their system and benefit from 0% VAT and export payments.

Do I have to apply for 0% VAT?

No. It applies automatically to solar panels and batteries fitted to homes, and it's already reflected in the price we quote.

How do I get Smart Export Guarantee payments?

After your install you receive an MCS certificate. You choose an export tariff from a supplier, send them the certificate, and they pay you for the power your smart meter records going back to the grid.

Can you check what I qualify for?

Yes. Tell us your postcode and a little about your household when you enquire, and we'll tell you which schemes are open to you.
