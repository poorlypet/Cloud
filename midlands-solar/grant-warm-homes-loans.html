Government funding

Warm Homes Plan loans

The government's Warm Homes Plan includes government-backed loans, at reduced interest, to help homeowners pay for solar panels and battery storage.

Who it's for

Homeowners who don't qualify for a grant

What it is

A reduced-rate loan

Offered through

Participating lenders

How it works

Three steps.

01

Get your price

Start with a quote so you know what your system costs.

02

We check what's open

The loans are offered through participating lenders. We'll tell you what is available when you enquire.

03

Apply to the lender

The lender carries out its own checks and sets the terms.

Who it's for

Who it's aimed at.

The loans are for households that can't get a grant but would rather not pay in one go.

  • Owner-occupiers, and private landlords
  • Solar panels, battery storage and heat pumps
  • Subject to the lender's own checks
  • Details are still being rolled out, so ask us for the latest
Check if I qualify →

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free money?

No. It is a loan that you repay, at a lower interest rate than a normal loan because the government subsidises it.

Is it open now?

The scheme is being introduced in stages through participating lenders. Ask us and we'll tell you where it stands when you enquire.

Can I use your finance instead?

Yes. See our finance page for the ways to spread the cost with us.
