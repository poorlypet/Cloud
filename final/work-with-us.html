Brand and product partnerships

For brands and makers

Have a high-quality product that could help dogs with common conditions or improve wellbeing? We'd love to hear from you.

  • Carefully vetted before we list it
  • Featured across our site
  • Long-term partnership potential

We say no to far more brands than we say yes to.

What we look for in a brand

  1. It helps with a real dog health needFrom recovery support to everyday wellbeing.
  2. Quality and safety come firstWe never compromise on safety or quality.
  3. A clear benefit for dogs and their ownersWe only partner where there is one.
  4. We would give it to MarbleIf we would not give it to Marble, our Dachshund, we will not sell it.
